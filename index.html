Market Maker Game!

Learn how stock trading works — set your prices, make money!

What is a Market Maker?

Imagine you run a lemonade stand, but instead of lemonade, you buy and sell stocks (tiny pieces of big companies like Apple or Google).

  • BID price = the price you're willing to BUY a stock for (like saying "I'll pay $148 for Apple stock")
  • ASK price = the price you're willing to SELL a stock for (like saying "I'll sell Apple stock for $152")
  • SPREAD = the gap between your Buy and Sell price. This is how you make money!
Example: Apple stock is worth about $150.
You set your BID (buy) at $148 and your ASK (sell) at $152.
If someone sells to you at $148, then someone else buys from you at $152…
You just made $4 profit! That's your spread! 🎉

But be careful! If you set your prices too far from the real price, nobody will trade with you. If you set them too close, you won't make much money. And if the stock price moves against you, you could lose money on the shares you're holding!

How This Game Works

  • There are 5 players — you and 4 computer players
  • Each round, a real stock is shown with its current price
  • You set your BID and ASK prices
  • Random traders come and buy/sell from whoever has the best prices
  • After 10 rounds, the player with the most money wins!
  • Everyone starts with $10,000 cash
