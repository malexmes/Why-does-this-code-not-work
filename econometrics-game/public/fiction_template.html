Chapter 1 · The receipts

Lantern is an invented charity and Big Splash is its invented sponsored swim. Every weekly figure on this site is made up: sign-ups, spend by channel and product, the weekly split by driver, brand consideration and new monthly givers, January 2023 to October 2025, were all generated from a known model plus noise so that the game has an answer to check you against. Sponsorship income is counted at £80 per sign-up. No real charity, agency or dataset is shown here.
